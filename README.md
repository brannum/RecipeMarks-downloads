# RecipeMarks for Linux

A local-first recipe organizer with recipe import, cooking views, grocery and pantry lists, meal planning, and portable backups.

## Download 2.0.1

These installers are for **64-bit Intel/AMD Linux desktops (x86-64 / amd64)**, not ARM64 or 32-bit systems.

## Your data and account

RecipeMarks Free Local works without an account. Hosted RecipeMarks Plus services are not for sale in this release. Linux downloads do not include Apple in-app purchases or unlock hosted account services.

Export a backup before changing package formats or moving between machines. Snap and direct packages may keep separate profiles; transfer through the app's supported export/import workflow.
